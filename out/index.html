How it works

Three steps, the same for every landing site.

Compare

Temperature, terrain, dust, ice, sunlight and more are measured on both worlds and compared property by property.

Rank and route

Every place on Earth gets a match percentage. The best ones are joined into a field trip where each stop tests or double-checks something.

Stay honest

Where Earth cannot stand in, such as near-vacuum pressure or lunar cold, the page says so and leaves it out of the score.

Pick a landing site

Each card opens the field trip for that site on the map. The percentage is how alike its best Earth site is.
